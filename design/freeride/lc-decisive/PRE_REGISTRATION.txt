PRE-REGISTERED CLASSIFICATION OF BUREAU FIELDS
Written BEFORE any horse-race ranking was computed. 2026-09-11.

Rule used: a field is "OTHER LENDERS' DECISION" if the number on the credit file is
set by a third-party lender's underwriting act - a limit they granted, an account they
approved, or the timing of such an approval. A field is "BORROWER'S OWN STATE" if the
number is set by what the borrower did with the credit or by their own circumstances -
balances drawn, utilisation, income, delinquency, public records, and their own
shopping behaviour (inquiries are the borrower applying, not a lender approving).
"MIXED" is used where a lender-granted number is netted against a borrower-driven one.

--- DECISION RULE FIXED IN ADVANCE ---
If log(total_bc_limit) is mid-pack among ALL bureau fields by absolute realized
net-return spread, the honest conclusion is COARSE GRADE (Lending Club's 35-notch
published grade is a coarse summary of its own model and many bureau fields beat it
inside a grade), NOT free-riding / brand halo.
If instead the OTHER-LENDERS'-DECISION class systematically occupies the top of the
ranking and the BORROWER'S-OWN-STATE class does not, and total_bc_limit is near the
top of its class with the sign that more third-party limit means lower loss, that is
a FREE-RIDING finding.
acc_open_past_24mths is the purest "other lenders just approved this person" proxy;
if its sign says more recent third-party approvals predict WORSE realized cash, the
simple free-riding reading is contradicted regardless of where total_bc_limit ranks.
