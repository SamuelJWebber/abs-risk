VERDICT
==============================================================================
THIS IS A COARSE-GRADE FINDING, NOT A FREE-RIDING FINDING.

The one-sentence answer: inside sub_grade x issue_year x 20-point FICO band, on
realized cash, log(total_bc_limit) ranks 42nd of 47 bureau fields (Q5-Q1 =
+$0.254 per $100, clustered t = +2.25, ~+20 bps/yr, NOT monotone), the
pre-registered "limits granted" sub-class it belongs to is the WORST of the four
sub-kinds (median rank 38.5, mean |spread| $0.52 vs $0.99 for borrower's-own-state
fields, and its four members split 2 positive / 2 negative), the strongest field on
the file is acc_open_past_24mths at -$4.74 per $100 (t = -33.9, ~-371 bps/yr,
perfectly monotone) with the sign OPPOSITE to free-riding, and applying the
breakpoints ex ante buys +4 bps/yr with the sign flipping across vintages.

--- HOW EACH REFEREE OBJECTION LANDED ---
1. LGD. Settled and the referee is right. Realized LGD on this sample is 51.6% of
   original principal, dollar-weighted, net of recoveries (51.7% simple mean over
   82,728 charged-off loans). 100% LGD overstated loss by 1.94x. The referee's 57%
   is the right order of magnitude; my 51.6% is on the mature 36-month 2012-2015
   sample specifically. This run makes no LGD assumption at all - it uses cash.
2. Annualisation divisor. Settled and the referee is right that 1.5 was wrong. The
   MEASURED interest-earning life on this sample is 1.278 years
   ($1,124,416,034 of interest received / $879,935,455 of principal-weighted APR
   exposure), so 1.5 was 1.17x too large. Every annualised number here is labelled
   as an approximation.
3. Counts vs dollars. Settled and the referee is right about the magnitude:
   Q1 median loan $7,650 vs Q5 median $17,350 - a 2.3x size difference. The outcome
   here is dollars per $100 of original principal, so the two are commensurable.
   Principal-weighting rather than loan-weighting moves Q1 from +7.94 to +7.43 and
   Q5 from +8.18 to +8.46, i.e. it WIDENS the raw gap to +1.04/$100 - but that raw
   gap is before any cell control; inside the cell it is +0.254.
4. FICO balance. Settled. With the 20-point FICO band in the cell, the residual
   Q5-Q1 FICO gap is +1.00 point (raw gap in this sample is +0.98 - the sample is
   already close to balanced once mature vintages are used). The headline is
   therefore FICO-balanced by construction.
5. THE DECISIVE OBJECTION. Confirmed and stronger than the referee stated.
   total_bc_limit is rank 42 of 47 by realized net-return spread, not 14th of 25.
   Top five: acc_open_past_24mths (-$4.74/$100), num_tl_op_past_12m (-$3.72),
   mths_since_recent_bc (+$2.68), mo_sin_rcnt_tl (+$2.68), inq_last_6mths (-$2.13).
   The referee's specific numbers differ from mine (I get avg_cur_bal +$1.82,
   mort_acc +$1.35, tot_hi_cred_lim +$1.22, annual_inc +$1.75, acc_open_past_24mths
   -$4.74) because of the cell definition, but the ORDERING and the conclusion match.
6. The internal contradiction. Confirmed. acc_open_past_24mths is #1 and its sign is
   backwards for free-riding: Q1 (median 1 account opened) earns +$10.17 per $100,
   Q5 (median 9) earns +$5.38, charge-off rises 11.6% to 17.3%, and the within-cell
   logistic gives +0.586 log-odds top-vs-bottom at clustered z = +42.6. More recent
   third-party approvals predicts MORE loss.
7. Clustered SEs. Done throughout, clustered on the cell. 585 clusters at the
   headline. Clustering inflates the headline SE by 1.22x (0.0921 to 0.1126), which
   is enough to take the headline t from +2.75 to +2.25.
8. Ex-ante implementability. The claim does NOT survive. Forward-applied
   breakpoints: 2013 +$0.068 (t +0.26), 2014 +$0.767 (t +4.48), 2015 -$0.356
   (t -2.50). N-weighted average +$0.054/$100 = +4 bps/yr against the in-sample
   +$0.254 = +20 bps/yr. The sign flips. By contrast acc_open_past_24mths delivers
   -$4.56/$100 = -357 bps/yr ex ante, essentially unchanged from in-sample.

--- WHAT IS STILL TRUE AFTER ALL OF THIS ---
(a) There IS residual, real, dollar-denominated risk ordering inside Lending Club's
    35-notch grade x FICO band. Many bureau fields deliver 100-400 bps/yr of
    realized net-return spread inside a cell. That is a coarse-grade finding:
    LC's published grade is a lossy summary of LC's own model.
(b) The strongest signals are credit-SEEKING intensity and recency - how many
    accounts were opened lately and how recently - not the SIZE of the credit lines
    other lenders granted. The mechanism is "this borrower is currently levering up",
    not "other lenders vouched for this borrower".
(c) The pre-registered class test superficially favours OTHER-LENDER fields (median
    rank 19.5 vs 28.5 for borrower's-own-state, P(other-lender outranks borrower)
    = 0.667), but that win is carried entirely by approval-COUNT and approval-TIMING
    fields whose sign says more/recent approvals = worse. The LIMITS-GRANTED
    sub-class, which is the actual free-riding construct, is the worst group in the
    table. So the class test does not rescue the free-riding reading; it relocates
    the signal to a construct with the opposite interpretation.
(d) total_bc_limit is not zero. It is positive and significant at t = +2.25 in the
    headline, it strengthens to +$0.631 (t = +6.74) once the cell also holds the
    acc_open_past_24mths quintile fixed, it is +$0.575 (t +4.59) in grade A and
    +$0.540 (t +3.25) in grade B, and it is +$2.42 (t +4.14, monotone) on the
    60-month book. It is a small true effect concentrated in the best grades. It is
    simply not a distinctive one - on the 60-month book it ranks 24th of 42, and
    acc_open_past_24mths is again #1 at -$10.57/$100 with the same backwards sign.
