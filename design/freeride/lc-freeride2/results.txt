Formula: annualised loss rate ~= cumulative charge-off over loan life / 1.5,
  where 1.5 years is the approximate average life of a 36-month amortising
  loan (weighted-average life of level payments with prepayment ignored is
  ~1.55y; we use 1.5). Charge-off here is the share of LOANS charged off, not
  the share of principal lost, so this is an upper bound on nothing and a
  rough proxy: it ignores recoveries, prepayment, partial repayment before
  default (loss given default < 100% of original balance), and timing.
