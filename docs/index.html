abs-risk

Consumer credit risk from public SEC filings. Every number traces to a filing.

The American Express card trust loses less than Synchrony's. Toyota's public auto loan pool loses less than Santander's at the same credit score. Either those lenders picked people who do not fall behind, or the same people fall behind and pay that account first. The loss rate cannot tell the two apart, and almost every public comparison stops there. Everything here is one securitised trust per card issuer and one or two public deals per auto lender, not the lenders' whole books.

What the filings can say, and what they cannot
Issuers with low losses have far fewer accounts that fall behind, in cards and in auto loans. Both stories predict that. Public filings cannot say whether their late accounts are rescued more or less often, which is where the stories differ, so they do not settle the question.
