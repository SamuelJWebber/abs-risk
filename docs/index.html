abs-risk

Consumer credit risk from public SEC filings. Two questions, two pages, every number traceable to a filing.

Auto loans: same score, different lender
Loan-level data from public auto ABS. Cumulative charge-off by score bucket and lender with prepayment as a competing risk, a discrete-time hazard model for lender and loan-size effects at fixed score, and regression discontinuity at lender pricing cutoffs. Card trusts: charge-off against what the score mix predicts
Monthly 10-D charge-offs for the six live card master trusts against a prediction from each pool's FICO mix under three published loss-by-tier shapes, and a trust-month panel regression.

The question that started this was whether payment hierarchy, which debt a person pays first, can be estimated from public data. It cannot: no public dataset observes one person across products. These two pages are the nearest honest neighbours, and each says on its face what it can and cannot support.

Source and methods: github.com/SamuelJWebber/abs-risk. Data: SEC EDGAR (Forms 10-D, 424B, ABS-EE), CFPB Consumer Credit Card Market Report figure data.
